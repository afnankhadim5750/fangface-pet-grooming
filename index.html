First visit?

Your pet's first visit, made easy

New to Fangface? Book by phone on 07547 780216, then drop off at 5 Bonvilston Road, Pontypridd. We groom one-on-one and give you a collection time when you arrive — a full groom usually takes 1.5–2.5 hours.

  • Puppies from 12 weeks (after vaccinations) start with a short, treat-heavy intro groom
  • Matted coat? We will talk it through first — we never de-mat painfully, and comfort comes first
  • Need to rearrange? Just give us 24 hours' notice and we will find another slot

Open Tue–Fri 9am–5pm, Sat 10am–4:30pm. Closed Mondays & Sundays (even groomers need a lie-in).

Why Fangface
