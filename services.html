Good to know

The fine print (the friendly kind)

How long does it take?

A full groom usually takes 1.5–2.5 hours depending on breed and coat. We'll give you a collection time when you drop off — plenty of time for a wander around Pontypridd.

Matted coats

Severely matted coats may need clipping short for your pet's comfort — we'll always discuss it with you first, and never de-mat painfully.

Cancellations

Life happens! Just give us 24 hours' notice if you need to rearrange and we'll happily find you another slot — no drama.
